## 1. Executive Summary & Qualitative Methodology

This document defines the semi-structured post-test interview protocol, verbatim bilingual questioning guide, response capture matrix, and thematic coding methodology for the LiveLift V3 Product Validation Program.

### 1.1 Research Objective & Qualitative Stance
While quantitative telemetry ($T_{\text{detect}}$, $T_{\text{decision}}$, $V_{\text{anchor}}$, NASA-TLX) captures second-level behavioral efficiency, qualitative debriefing uncovers the underlying cognitive models, trust dynamics, perceived agency, and commercial viability of the LiveLift Operations Desk compared with the Google Sheets + Chat baseline.

Key qualitative objectives:
1. **Explain the Latency Gap:** Understand *why* operators detected deficits faster or hesitated during recovery decisions in each condition.
2. **Evaluate the Multi-Window Bottleneck:** Unpack the subjective cognitive friction of managing OBS, Seller Center, Google Sheets, and Zalo simultaneously.
3. **Assess Talent Pacing & Glance Friction:** Evaluate the on-camera host's emotional safety, glance budget strain, and verbal delivery smoothness under atomic LiveLift cues vs. unstructured chat messages.
4. **Determine Commercial Adoption Viability:** Measure voluntary tool adoption via a forced-choice commitment probe: *"If you were directing tomorrow's real live sales broadcast, which tool would you voluntarily mandate for your studio, and why?"*

## 2. Interview Structure & Administration Guidelines

The debriefing interview is administered immediately following the completion of Trial 2 and the post-test NASA-TLX workload survey. Total duration is approximately **15 to 20 minutes**.

```
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                               POST-TEST DEBRIEFING INTERVIEW PHASES                                |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                                                                                    |
|  [ PHASE 1: JOINT WARM-UP & GENERAL IMPRESSION (3–4 min) ]                                         |
|  - Setting rapport; validating broadcast performance; unguided open reactions to both trials.      |
|                                                                                                    |
|  [ PHASE 2: OPERATOR IN-DEPTH PROBES (6–7 min) ]                                                   |
|  - Theme 1: Cognitive Load & Split Attention (Mental arithmetic vs interface cues).               |
|  - Theme 2: Schedule Awareness & Panic Perception (Deficit detection & alarms).                   |
|  - Theme 3: Recovery Feasibility & Autonomy (Decision speed, constraint trust, manual override).  |
|                                                                                                    |
|  [ PHASE 3: HOST IN-DEPTH PROBES (4–5 min) ]                                                       |
|  - Theme 4: Host Coordination & Cue Clarity (Glance budget, reading strain, verbal flow).          |
|                                                                                                    |
|  [ PHASE 4: JOINT SYNTHESIS & FORCED-CHOICE ADOPTION (4–5 min) ]                                   |
|  - Theme 5: Post-Show Trust & Commercial Utility (Plan-vs-Actual audit, Next LIVE planning).       |
|  - Forced-Choice Commercial Adoption Probe & Keep/Change/Remove triage.                           |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 2.1 Interviewer Code of Conduct
1. **Neutrality & Non-Defense:** The interviewer must maintain clinical neutrality. Never defend LiveLift prototype limitations, explain away bugs, or criticize spreadsheet performance. 
2. **Laddering Technique:** When participants provide brief answers (e.g., *"It felt easier"*), probe deeper using laddering prompts: *"What specifically on the screen made it feel that way?"*, *"Can you recall the moment the Retinol stock dropped to zero?"*
3. **Grounded in Behavioral Observations:** Reference specific incidents recorded by Observer 1 and Observer 2 during the trials (e.g., *"During Trial 1 at the 6-minute mark, you took a deep breath and looked between windows—what was happening in your head right then?"*).

## 3. The 5 Core Qualitative Themes

```
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                   THE 5 CORE QUALITATIVE THEMES                                    |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Theme 1: Cognitive Load & Split Attention    | Mental math strain, 4-window juggling, attention lag|
| Theme 2: Schedule Awareness & Panic Burden   | Peripheral alarm visibility, deadline predictability|
| Theme 3: Recovery Feasibility & Autonomy     | Feasibility of suggested choices, perceived control |
| Theme 4: Host Coordination & Cue Clarity     | Glance budget, reading fatigue, on-camera delivery  |
| Theme 5: Post-Show Trust & Commercial Choice | Reconstruction speed, Next LIVE reuse, forced choice|
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Theme 1: Cognitive Load & Split Attention
- **Focus:** Evaluates the mental effort of recalculating downstream time budgets while managing Seller Center product pins and chat backchannels.
- **Key Probe Dimensions:**
  * Mental arithmetic strain under broadcast pressure.
  * Friction of window switching (OBS vs Seller Center vs Sheets vs Zalo).
  * Multi-tasking panic during sudden inventory or network events.

### Theme 2: Schedule Awareness & Panic Perception
- **Focus:** Assesses how easily the operator maintained situational awareness of hard promotion deadlines.
- **Key Probe Dimensions:**
  * Peripheral visibility of schedule deficit alarms (Red cell `Col R` vs LiveLift alert banner).
  * Feeling of control vs feeling surprised by upcoming hard promotional drops.
  * Subjective panic levels during active pitch overruns.

### Theme 3: Recovery Feasibility & Autonomy
- **Focus:** Evaluates how the operator formulated recovery trade-offs when an overrun occurred.
- **Key Probe Dimensions:**
  * Ease of determining available buffer without violating contractual floor durations.
  * Perceived utility of explicit recovery choices vs manual mental calculation.
  * Sense of operator autonomy: Did the system assist or feel like an intrusive black box?

### Theme 4: Host Coordination & Cue Clarity
- **Focus:** Examines the on-camera talent's cognitive ergonomics and visual focus during broadcast operations.
- **Key Probe Dimensions:**
  * Glance budget: Time required to read and comprehend instructions while speaking live.
  * Textual density: Atomic cues ($\le 5$ words) vs multi-sentence Zalo messages.
  * Frequency of verbal hesitations, speech stumbles, or eye drift away from the camera lens.

### Theme 5: Post-Show Trust & Commercial Utility
- **Focus:** Measures post-live operational reconciliation efficiency and real-world commercial commitment.
- **Key Probe Dimensions:**
  * Confidence in Plan-vs-Actual duration reconstruction without re-watching stream video.
  * Willingness to adopt Next LIVE learning patches across multi-day campaigns.
  * The definitive **Forced-Choice Commercial Decision**: Which tool would the team mandate for their live studio tomorrow?

## 4. Structured Bilingual Interview Guide

### Phase 1: Joint Warm-up & General Impression (3–4 minutes)

#### Probe Q1.1: General Experience Comparison
* **Vietnamese (VN):** *"Cảm ơn hai bạn đã hoàn thành cả hai phiên chạy thử nghiệm. Nhìn chung, khi so sánh giữa phiên sử dụng Bảng tính Google Sheets + Zalo và phiên sử dụng Bàn điều hành LiveLift, bạn cảm thấy phiên nào diễn ra trơn tru và dễ kiểm soát nhịp độ hơn? Điều gì tạo nên sự khác biệt lớn nhất?"*
* **English (EN):** *"Thank you both for completing both test sessions. Looking across both trials, comparing the Google Sheets + Zalo workflow against the LiveLift Operations Desk, which trial felt smoother and more controllable in terms of broadcast pacing? What created the biggest difference?"*
* **Laddering Follow-up:** *"Có khoảnh khắc nào trong một trong hai phiên mà bạn cảm thấy hoàn toàn mất kiểm soát thời gian không?"* (*"Was there any moment where you felt completely out of control of the schedule?"*)

---

### Phase 2: Operator In-Depth Probes (6–7 minutes)

#### Probe Q2.1: Cognitive Load & Split Attention (Theme 1)
* **Vietnamese (VN):** *"Khi xảy ra tình huống Serum bị kéo dài thời gian (overrun) ở phút thứ 6, trong phiên dùng Bảng tính, bạn đã phải làm những bước tính toán nào trong đầu để biết có kịp giờ Flash Deal lúc 09:00 không? Bạn cảm thấy việc vừa nhìn Seller Center vừa tính toán trong Sheet gây căng thẳng như thế nào so với khi nhìn màn hình LiveLift?"*
* **English (EN):** *"When the Serum overrun occurred at minute 6, in the Google Sheets session, what mental calculations did you have to perform to determine if you would still hit the 09:00 Flash Deal? How stressful was juggling Seller Center alongside spreadsheet math compared to looking at the LiveLift desk?"*
* **Laddering Follow-up:** *"Bạn có bao giờ sợ rằng nếu mình sửa nhầm một ô thì toàn bộ công thức tính giờ bên dưới sẽ bị hỏng không?"* (*"Were you worried that editing a cell by accident would break downstream formulas?"*)

#### Probe Q2.2: Schedule Awareness & Alarm Perception (Theme 2)
* **Vietnamese (VN):** *"Khi phiên live bị trễ tiến độ, việc nhìn ô màu đỏ ở Cột R trên Google Sheets và việc nhìn thẻ cảnh báo thâm hụt (deficit alert) trên LiveLift mang lại cảm giác khác nhau ra sao? Bạn phát hiện ra nguy cơ trễ giờ ở công cụ nào nhanh hơn và tự tin hơn?"*
* **English (EN):** *"When the broadcast fell behind schedule, how did noticing the red cell in Column R on Google Sheets compare to seeing the deficit alert card on LiveLift? Which tool allowed you to detect the schedule risk faster and with more confidence?"*
* **Laddering Follow-up:** *"Lúc nhìn ô màu đỏ trên Sheet, bạn có biết ngay lập tức mình phải làm gì không, hay phải mất thời gian nhìn lại các dòng sản phẩm tiếp theo?"* (*"When you saw the red cell in Sheets, did you immediately know what action to take, or did you have to inspect upcoming rows?"*)

#### Probe Q2.3: Recovery Feasibility & Perceived Control (Theme 3)
* **Vietnamese (VN):** *"Khi phải xử lý sự cố cháy hàng (stockout) hoặc trễ tiến độ, các lựa chọn điều chỉnh (như rút ngắn Toner, bỏ qua sản phẩm, hoặc bù thời gian) trên LiveLift có thực tế và dễ thực hiện không? Bạn cảm thấy mình vẫn là người kiểm soát quyết định hay hệ thống đang áp đặt bạn?"*
* **English (EN):** *"When dealing with the stockout or pacing deficit, how realistic and actionable were the adjustment options (compressing Toner, skipping, or extending) in LiveLift? Did you feel you retained operational control, or did the system feel prescriptive?"*
* **Laddering Follow-up:** *"Nếu LiveLift đề xuất một phương án mà bạn không đồng ý, nút bấm chọn thủ công ('Choose next') có đủ linh hoạt để bạn xử lý theo ý mình không?"* (*"If LiveLift suggested an option you disagreed with, was the manual override ('Choose next') flexible enough for your needs?"*)

### Phase 3: Host In-Depth Probes (4–5 minutes)

#### Probe Q3.1: Host Glance Budget & Information Ergonomics (Theme 4)
* **Vietnamese (VN):** *"Khi bạn đang say sưa thuyết trình và demo sản phẩm trên sóng, việc nhìn vào màn hình tablet hiển thị tín hiệu LiveLift (đồng hồ đếm ngược, tên mã hàng, lệnh ngắn $\le 5$ chữ) so với việc đọc tin nhắn trong nhóm Zalo ảnh hưởng đến sự tập trung của bạn như thế nào?"*
* **English (EN):** *"While pitching and demonstrating products on camera, how did glancing at the LiveLift cue tablet (countdown timer, SKU name, brief instruction $\le 5$ words) compare to reading messages in the Zalo chat group in terms of visual and cognitive distraction?"*
* **Laddering Follow-up:** *"Đã có lần nào bạn bị vấp lời, ngập ngừng hoặc bị đơ mắt nhìn vào màn hình khi cố đọc tin nhắn Zalo chưa?"* (*"Did you experience any speech stumbles, hesitations, or frozen stares while trying to read Zalo messages?"*)

#### Probe Q3.2: Cue Actionability & Confidence (Theme 4)
* **Vietnamese (VN):** *"Khi có sự cố bất ngờ như mã Kem Dưỡng bị hết hàng đột ngột hoặc Seller Center bị lag mạng, bạn cảm thấy nhận tín hiệu nào giúp bạn ứng biến tự tin hơn trên sóng: một dòng tin nhắn Zalo dài hay một chỉ dẫn trực quan rõ ràng trên LiveLift?"*
* **English (EN):** *"During unexpected incidents like the sudden Retinol stockout or Seller Center pin lag, which signal allowed you to pivot more confidently on stream: a detailed Zalo text message or a clear visual cue on LiveLift?"*
* **Laddering Follow-up:** *"Bạn có cảm giác an tâm hơn khi biết chính xác còn bao nhiêu giây trước khi phiên Flash Deal mở bán không?"* (*"Did you feel more secure knowing exactly how many seconds remained before the Flash Deal unlocked?"*)

### Phase 4: Joint Synthesis & Commercial Choice (4–5 minutes)

#### Probe Q4.1: Post-Show Plan-vs-Actual Trust (Theme 5)
* **Vietnamese (VN):** *"Sau khi phiên live kết thúc, khi nhìn vào màn hình Tổng kết (Review / Replay) của LiveLift so với việc nhìn vào bảng Google Sheets đã bị ghi đè dữ liệu thực tế, bạn cảm thấy bên nào giúp bạn giải trình chính xác những gì đã diễn ra trên sóng (mã nào bị trễ, mã nào bị cắt) dễ dàng hơn?"*
* **English (EN):** *"After the broadcast concluded, comparing LiveLift's Review / Replay workspace against the Google Sheet with overwritten actuals, which tool gave you higher confidence in reconstructing what actually happened on air for post-show debriefs and brand audits?"*

#### Probe Q4.2: The Forced-Choice Commercial Adoption Probe (Theme 5)
* **Vietnamese (VN):** > **"Nếu ngày mai studio của hai bạn có một phiên Mega Live thật sự kéo dài 2 tiếng với doanh số cam kết hàng trăm triệu đồng, và bạn chỉ được chọn DUY NHẤT một hệ thống vận hành để điều phối phiên live: bạn sẽ chọn Bảng tính Google Sheets + Zalo hiện tại hay Bàn điều hành LiveLift? Vì sao?"**
* **English (EN):** > **"If your studio had a real 2-hour Mega Live tomorrow with committed brand revenue of hundreds of millions of VND, and you were forced to choose EXACTLY ONE operational tool to manage the broadcast: would you choose your current Google Sheets + Zalo workflow or the LiveLift Operations Desk? Why?"**

#### Probe Q4.3: Keep / Change / Remove Feature Triage
* **Vietnamese (VN):**
  - *Keep (Giữ lại):* *"Tính năng nào trên LiveLift mà bạn cảm thấy hữu ích nhất và bắt buộc phải có?"*
  - *Change (Thay đổi):* *"Phần nào trên giao diện khiến bạn khó chịu hoặc cần cải tiến ngay lập tức?"*
  - *Remove (Bỏ đi):* *"Có chi tiết nào thừa thãi, gây rối mắt mà nhóm phát triển nên loại bỏ không?"*
* **English (EN):**
  - *Keep:* *"Which feature in LiveLift was most valuable and non-negotiable for your daily operations?"*
  - *Change:* *"What interface element caused friction and needs immediate improvement?"*
  - *Remove:* *"Is there any unnecessary clutter that the development team should remove?"*

## 6. Qualitative Analysis & Coding Protocol

To transform verbatim interview narratives into defensible empirical findings:

### 6.1 Thematic Coding Procedures
1. **Verbatim Transcription:** Audio recordings of debrief interviews are transcribed verbatim in Vietnamese, preserving authentic colloquial broadcast terminology (*cháy hàng, đơ máy, vấp lời, ghim giỏ, thâm hụt, giờ vàng*).
2. **Two-Coder Independent Extraction:** Two independent qualitative researchers review each transcript and tag statements according to the 5 Core Themes:
   - `TH1_COGNITIVE_LOAD`: Statements addressing mental arithmetic, memory strain, multi-window juggling.
   - `TH2_SCHEDULE_AWARENESS`: Statements addressing deadline predictability, peripheral alarms, panic.
   - `TH3_RECOVERY_AUTONOMY`: Statements addressing choice feasibility, system flexibility, manual overrides.
   - `TH4_HOST_COORDINATION`: Statements addressing reading glance budget, teleprompter eye drift, verbal delivery.
   - `TH5_COMMERCIAL_TRUST`: Statements addressing PVA reconstruction, post-show reporting, forced-choice adoption.
3. **Sentiment Polarity Scoring:**
   - **`+1` (Favorable / Advantageous):** Expresses reduced effort, increased confidence, visual clarity, or operational superiority.
   - **`0` (Neutral / Equivalent):** Expresses parity or indifference between conditions.
   - **`-1` (Unfavorable / Detrimental):** Expresses confusion, cognitive friction, panic, distrust, or failure.
4. **Inter-Coder Reliability Audit:**
   - The two coders calculate Cohen's Kappa across coded segments:
     $$\kappa_{\text{qual}} \ge 0.80$$
   - Discrepancies are arbitrated in conference with the Lead Qualitative Researcher.
